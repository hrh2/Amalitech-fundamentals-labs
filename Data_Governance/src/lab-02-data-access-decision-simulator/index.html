Ticket 01 / 03 — Marketing Campaign

The referral campaign request

From: Sarah Owusu, Marketing Manager · Current access: Internal only
"I need the full student database (names, emails, phone numbers, course enrollments) to launch our new referral campaign. This is urgent — campaign starts Friday!"
Emails — Confidential Names — Internal Enrollments — Internal
Your decision:
Reference decision
Conditionally Approve
Lifecycle stage
Share (internal data leaving its origin system for a new use)
Justification
Sarah's Internal clearance does not cover the Confidential email field. Least privilege means granting the campaign only the fields it needs — not the full database.
Safeguards to attach
  • Scope access to names + enrollment segment only; exclude raw emails/phone numbers
  • Route campaign sends through a masked mailer service, not a raw export
  • Get Data Owner (Registrar) and Privacy lead sign-off before release
  • Time-box the access to the campaign window
Ticket 02 / 03 — Analytics Partnership

The third-party AWS credentials request

From: David Mensah, Head of Product · Compliance context: Ghana DPA (Act 843)
"I've signed a partnership with DataInsights Inc. (US-based) to analyze our student learning patterns. They need access to our AWS database to run their algorithms. Login credentials attached."
Activity logs — Internal Student profiles — Confidential
Your decision:
Reference decision
Deny (as requested) → Conditional (with proper controls)
Lifecycle stage
Share (cross-border transfer to a third-party processor)
Compliance red flags
  • Sharing raw AWS login credentials bypasses access logging entirely — never share database credentials directly
  • Cross-border transfer of Confidential profiles under Ghana DPA needs a lawful transfer mechanism and a signed Data Processing Agreement
  • No evidence of student consent or legitimate-interest basis for this specific use
Controls required before sharing
  • Revoke the shared credentials; issue DataInsights a scoped, read-only role instead
  • Execute a Data Processing Agreement covering the DPA's cross-border transfer terms
  • Anonymize or pseudonymize profiles before export; share activity logs only where feasible
  • Document lawful basis and notify Legal/Data Protection Officer
Ticket 03 / 03 — Archive & Deletion

The right-to-be-forgotten request

From: Comfort Asante, Customer Support Lead · Status: Account inactive, no pending payments
"A student, James Boateng, has requested full deletion of his account and all associated data per his 'right to be forgotten.' He completed his last course 6 months ago and has no pending payments. How should I proceed?"
All student data — Confidential
Your decision:
Reference decision
Conditional (partial deletion)
Lifecycle stage
Destroy (with a retention carve-out)
Legal obligations under Ghana DPA
The right to deletion exists but is not absolute — records needed for legal or tax compliance may be retained even after the deletion request is honored.
Process for Customer Support
  • Verify James's identity before acting
  • Delete profile data, course history, and marketing records
  • Retain only payment/financial records required by Ghanaian financial regulations, for the mandated retention period
  • Log the request and outcome for audit purposes
  • Confirm completion to the requester in writing
