01 — Risk Register

What the review found

Four linked risk areas, each with a concrete mitigation drawn from data governance fundamentals: minimization, consent, access control, and fairness monitoring.

Data Quality
Risk 01

Incomplete & inconsistent inputs

Missing income fields and inconsistent phone formats lead to inaccurate ML predictions and poor loan decisions.
FixInput validation rules, required fields, standardized preprocessing pipelines.
Legal & Compliance
Risk 02

No explicit consent

Sensitive personal data collected without consent violates Ghana's Data Protection Act (Act 843).
FixOpt-in consent management with audit logs; apply data minimization.
Bias & Fairness
Risk 03

Biased demographic training data

Historical socioeconomic inequalities reflected in training data drive unfair approvals or rejections.
FixFairness constraints in training; bias detection tooling; balanced retraining.
Reporting
Risk 04

No fairness visibility

Without a tracked metric, disparities in approval rates go unnoticed until they become a compliance issue.
FixTrack "Approval Rate by Demographic Group" continuously, visualized as a grouped bar chart.

04 — Review Summary

Reading the pipeline as a governance story

The review traced each pipeline stage against data lifecycle and classification principles. Excessive collection at intake violated data minimization; the absence of consent capture was the clearest compliance gap under Act 843. Classifying the dataset as Sensitive justified encryption, RBAC, and masking downstream.

Data quality issues surfaced at preprocessing, where incomplete and inconsistent inputs degraded model reliability — addressed through validation and standardization. Fairness concerns traced back to biased historical training data, which retraining and fairness constraints are intended to correct.

The proposed "Approval Rate by Demographic Group" metric, tracked continuously and visualized as a grouped bar chart, gives stakeholders a practical way to catch disparities early and keep the system accountable and explainable.
