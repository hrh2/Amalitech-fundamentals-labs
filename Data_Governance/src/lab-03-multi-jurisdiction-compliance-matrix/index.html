Same request, three legal realities.

Three customers ask ShopGhana to delete their data on the same day. Ghana's DPA, the EU's GDPR, and California's CCPA each answer differently.

01 — Deadline & Penalty Comparison

The clock and the cost differ by jurisdiction

Response windows are comparable in days. Penalty structures are not — GDPR scales with global revenue, CCPA is per-violation, and Ghana relies on regulatory sanctions rather than fixed fines.

CCPA figure shown as its standard 45-day window (extendable to 90 with notice).

Legal rights
Right to request deletion under Act 843 — but it is not absolute.
Data retention
Transaction and payment records retained for Ghanaian financial/tax compliance, typically several years.
Action steps

Legal rights
Strong right to erasure under GDPR Article 17 ("Right to be Forgotten").
Exemptions
Legal compliance obligations (e.g. tax records) and defense of legal claims.
Action steps
  • Verify identity
  • Delete personal data across all systems
  • Notify third-party processors
  • Confirm deletion to the user
